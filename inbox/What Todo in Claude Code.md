## The curse of productivity

As you described in the mail, it's easy to end up with a bunch of different software tools for what felt like different, and useful, aspects when you started with them. 

Like having Drafts for fast capture and on the fly markdown notetaking, Notion for projects with content sharing, Obsidian for so called knowledge management and Todoist and/or Remindersfor task management. Or maybe Trello. Etc. in finitum.

And then you realize that you need or at least want to connect some of them in some way to make them work together, seamlessly. Or to make yourself work more efficiently with...you. 

Like many, I had a setup similar to yours, and went through a similar process. But with a different result. Which I realized can be inspiring for others.
