# The Death of Medium

> Will A.I. be the death of Medium? Will the amount of slop soon exceed what anyone would pay for? And why are the Medium staff signaling that they are soft on A.I.? 

## It’s not Trust, it’s Authenticity
In a recent post, Medium is heralding an intellectually weak and faulty argument, implicitly to post-rationalize being soft on A.I.. It goes like this: We, the subscription payers and readers, do not care if a text is written by A.I. or not, we care if we can trust the text.

The comparision with writing for SEM isn't only misplaced, but also misinformed. Writers have had 'formats' to comply with as long as they have been earning their keep by that thread. One of the funniest examples of writing for a machine is when journalism changed because of the telegraph and the Civil War. The telegraph lines would get pulled down by war incidents, and writers filing stories learnt to change the structure from a three act drama to leading with the most important stuff and then having the rest in a receeding order. (Hence 'a lede'.) So they would make sure that the important infomation got through in case the lines got cut.
