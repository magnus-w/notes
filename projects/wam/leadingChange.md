# Leading Change

> How do you lead in times of great and fast change? 


## Hypotheses Based Thinking and Behaviour 

> The Scientific Method 

- The big change when it come to change, is the leaders are no longer required to know all the answers. 
- No longer required to tell us what to do, especially not in Knowledge Work where the workers know more than management.
- So what are they require to do? 
- Both when it comes to business strategy, product development, people development as well as their own development, they have to become hypothesis driven. 
- We all know Mary Wollstonecraft and the others from the Enlightenment and the core idea behind the scientific revolution.
- So in times of rapid change, we have less "pre-coocked" answers to what we should do and fewer "truths", and instead we need to formulate hypoytheses and verify them. 

## Being Comfortable Not Knowing the Answer
