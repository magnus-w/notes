# Open Spaces Invite - Draft

**Subject:** Ask the Coaches: An Open Space Event for Everyone in R&D

What's the difference in responsibilty between Flow and Value roles? How do we handle dependencies to other teams? 

Whether your team has worked in Flow Agile for a while or hasn't started yet, you probably have questions about it. Some are practical, like like the ones above. Others are larger: Why do we do PI Planning and how does it differ from what we used to do?

On [date] you can meet the coaches and get anwers. And this session is an Open Space, so you set the agenda. 

In the Teams meeting lobby, anyone can propose a topic. Then we split into breakout rooms, and you join the conversations that matter most to you. You get answers that fit your situation rather than a general presentation. You also hear how colleagues in other Solutions and ARTs have dealt with the same challenges. 

If your area is still waiting to be onboarded, it's a good chance to learn what's ahead and raise concerns early. No question is too basic and no only limitation is that we only deal with Flow Agile questions, not general organisation.

Join us on [date] from [time] to [time] CET via [link]. You don't need to prepare anything, but if a question has been on your mind, bring it along. 

Feel free to forward this invitation to colleagues in R&D who might be interested.
