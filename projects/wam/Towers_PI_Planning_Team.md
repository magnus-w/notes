# Towers PI Planning - Leadership Team Assesment

## 1. Pre-Planning Readiness
Backlog refinement, architectural runway, logistics, and materials ready before the event.
- Ddk

## 2. Vision & Strategic Alignment
Clarity of business context, customer/market input, and connection between ART vision and PI Objectives. (Primarily PM)
- Dks

## 3. Architecture & Technical Enablement
Presentation of architectural vision, enabler sequencing, and technical guardrails for teams. (Primarily SA)
- dk

## 4. Facilitation & Event Flow
Time management, energy in the room, breakout quality, and keeping teams productive across the two days. (Primarily RTE)
- Dks

## 5. Cross-ART/Team Coordination
Identification and negotiation of dependencies, interface alignment, and integration points between the three ARTs.
- Dsk

## 6. Risk & Impediment Management
Proactive surfacing of risks, quality of ROAMing, and ownership of mitigation actions.
- Dk

## 7. PI Objective Quality & Commitment
Objectives are SMART, realistically loaded, uncommitted objectives are used appropriately, and the confidence vote reflects genuine team buy-in.
- dk

## 8. Leadership Triad Collaboration
How well PM, RTE, and SA operated as a unified leadership team—supporting each other, communicating consistently, and presenting a single narrative.
- dk

## 9. Stakeholder Engagement
Business Owners were present and engaged, team participation was balanced, and feedback was actively solicited.
- dk
