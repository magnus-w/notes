## Why
The CoP is a peer group for PMs and POs across the three ARTs. It's a place to share experiences, compare how you handle challenges, such as prioritisation, Feature splitting, stakeholder pressure and dependencies across ARTs and Solutions. 

The idea is to be inspired by each others, borrow what works and agree on common practices. It is not training and there is no curriculum. The content comes from the members of the community.

## How a session works
Members bring current situation from their ART and everybody votes on which topics to tackle during the current session or team. The group asks questions and shares how they have handled something similar. 

Each participant leaves with one concrete thing to try before the next session
The next session starts with a short round on how those experiments went.

## Practicalities
Every second week, 60 minutes, on [day] in the middle of the sprint, to avoid conflicts with planning and reviews
